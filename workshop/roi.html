Under fee for service these strategies compete on cost per patient treated. Under a value-based contract money also turns on a population statistic: the share of the panel with a qualifying eye exam in the measurement year. Clear the gate and the system earns a reward per patient; miss it and it pays a penalty. Because that is a step function of everyone's results at once, it cannot live in the decision tree — it is computed from the run here. The cost-effectiveness analysis is deliberately left alone: incentive payments are a transfer between payer and provider, not a health-care resource, so they belong to the financial case rather than the ICER.

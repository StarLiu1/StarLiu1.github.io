⏱️ Step 5: Time Tradeoff

📖 Instructions (click to hide/show)

Compare 10 years in the worst outcome versus fewer years in a better outcome (then death). Drag the handle to adjust years. Click "≈ Equivalent" when both options feel equal. Utility = years ÷ 10.

⚠️ Important: TTO Assumption

Time Tradeoff (TTO) assumes that health outcomes are chronic — that is, they persist over time without significant change. TTO is most appropriate for conditions like:

  • Chronic diseases (diabetes, arthritis, heart disease)
  • Permanent disabilities or impairments
  • Stable health states that don't resolve on their own

TTO may be less appropriate for:

  • Acute, temporary conditions (common cold, minor injuries)
  • Conditions with highly variable progression
  • States that typically resolve or significantly improve

The worst-ranked outcome will be used as the reference (10 years).

⚖️ Step 6: Maximum False Positives Acceptable (MFPA)

📖 Instructions (click to hide/show)

MFPA (Maximum False Positives Acceptable) captures how many false positive diagnoses/treatments you would accept to avoid missing one true positive case (false negative).

This is particularly useful in diagnostic decision-making where:

  • False Negative (FN): Missing a true case (e.g., not treating someone who has disease)
  • False Positive (FP): Incorrectly treating someone without disease

A higher MFPA indicates you are more concerned about missing true cases and willing to accept more unnecessary treatments to avoid that.

🎯 The Tradeoff Question

Consider a diagnostic test or treatment decision. To avoid missing ONE person who truly has the condition (false negative), how many people WITHOUT the condition would you be willing to treat unnecessarily (false positives)?
